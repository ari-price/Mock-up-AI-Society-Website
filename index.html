Who We Are
Think critically about
what comes next
The purpose of the AI Society is to foster a community of students interested in the practical application of Artificial Intelligence, emphasizing the integration, advancement, and ethical implications of AI in business. We provide interactive, educational workshops, networking opportunities with industry professionals, and a collaborative environment for AI-driven projects — ensuring USD students are prepared for a future integrated with AI technology.

Frontier Research

Weekly deep dives into the latest papers, models, and breakthroughs. From foundation models to agentic architectures, we break it down together.

Build and Ship

Hands-on workshops and hack sessions where you build real things — autonomous agents, fine-tuned models, AI-powered tools. Learn by doing.

Conversations That Matter

Guest speakers, roundtable debates, and honest discussion about AI safety, ethics, job displacement, and what a world with autonomous systems looks like.
